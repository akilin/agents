Ask me questions until every pending decision, question and approval in this session has an answer. 
Include choices you made without asking me, where a wrong guess would matter.

Ask in rounds of numbered questions, each with your recommended answer and worded so "yes" accepts it. Hold back questions that depend on an answer still open.

If nothing is pending, say so in one line.
